从舵机编程到动作排练From servo programming to motion rehearsal

胡萝卜鹅沿用 TonyPi 上位机的关节编号、数值和逐帧编排方式，将它们放进可以旋转观察的 3D 工作台。动作、台词与停顿可以先在浏览器里反复调整，再进入真机排练。

Carrot Goose brings TonyPi joint values and frame editing into a 3D workbench. It helps compose gestures around dialogue, compare poses and revise timing before a physical rehearsal.

它在项目中的作用Its role in the project

排练时，我们需要比较“抬手”和“再次邀请”的幅度，让点头与网页回复之间留出停顿，也需要检查收手如何衔接下一段动作。这个工作台把这些选择变成可以播放、修改和保存的轨迹，减少只靠真机来回试动作的次数。

The workbench makes it easier to compare a first reach with a stronger invitation, leave a pause after a reply, and inspect how an arm returns before the next gesture. These choices become editable sequences that can be replayed and saved.

3D 预览帮助检查姿态与时间；接触、负载和实际关节限位仍需在真机上确认。
The preview supports pose and timing review. Contact, load and physical travel limits still require testing on the robot.
