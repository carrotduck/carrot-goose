# Third-party material

The browser bundles Three.js and OrbitControls under the MIT license; the license is in `apps/studio/vendor/LICENSE.three.txt`. Express and its dependencies carry their own licenses through npm.

TonyPi is a Hiwonder platform. Its SDK and factory action files are obtained from Hiwonder and remain subject to their terms. The importer allows a user to read their local action files. Factory motion binaries and generated voice recordings are not redistributed here.

The procedural robot model was built for this project from visual references. It is an approximation of the TonyPi mechanism.
